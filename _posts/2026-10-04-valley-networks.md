Ancient valley networks are among the clearest evidence that liquid water once flowed across the surface of Mars. I use digital elevation models derived from HRSC and CTX stereo imagery to measure valley geometry along trunk channels and tributaries.

Key questions:

* How deeply did valleys incise, and how does incision change downstream?
* Where are knickpoints located, and what do they record about base-level change and tributary junctions?
* How much material was eroded from valleys, and how does it compare with the volume preserved in downstream deposits such as fans and deltas?
