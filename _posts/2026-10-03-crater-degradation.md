Impact craters form with a predictable shape, so the difference between a fresh crater and a degraded one records how much material was later removed or deposited. I build a global inventory of degraded Noachian craters to estimate the total erosion volume and its spatial distribution, and to place constraints on erosion rates during the earliest period of Martian history.
