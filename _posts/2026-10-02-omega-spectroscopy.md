The OMEGA imaging spectrometer on Mars Express has mapped the surface of Mars in visible and near-infrared wavelengths. I develop tools to compute spectral parameters from OMEGA data and use them to identify surface materials, with a particular focus on detecting volcanic and impact glasses and separating their signatures from those of other iron-bearing minerals.
