I am a researcher in planetary science at The University of Hong Kong. My research uses orbital remote sensing and topographic data to understand the aqueous and erosional history of Mars.

Research Interests
======

* **Martian valley networks and fluvial erosion**: Mapping and measuring ancient river valleys with HRSC and CTX digital elevation models, and what their incision, knickpoints and eroded volumes tell us about early Mars hydrology.
* **Crater degradation and Noachian erosion**: Estimating how much material was removed from degraded craters across the planet, and what this means for erosion rates on early Mars.
* **Visible–infrared spectroscopy of the Martian surface**: Spectral parameter analysis of OMEGA data, including detection of volcanic and impact glasses.
* **Landscape evolution modeling**: Numerical simulation of how Martian landscapes respond to different climate and erosion scenarios.
